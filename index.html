Company Overview

A trusted ground management firm.

We act as our clients' trusted representative on the ground in Liberia — the local presence for owners, investors, and creatives who are building or holding assets from a distance, and need someone reliable to answer for them here.

Who We Are

Presence, on your behalf

A ground management firm built for the diaspora — we stand in for owners who can't be on-site, so nothing is left unattended.

Our Mission

Oversight you can trust from anywhere

To protect the investments of the diaspora and local creatives alike, delivering reliable oversight, transparent reporting, and dependable execution — so your property, projects, and contracts are always in safe hands, wherever you are in the world.

Sinkor Family Compound

An unoccupied family compound had fallen into disrepair while the owner was in the US. We restored the roofing and fencing, placed a caretaker, and now file a monthly report with photos.

"For the first time in years I know exactly what's happening at my parents' house without calling relatives to go check." Compound owner, Sinkor

After

Old Road Warehouse

A half-finished warehouse shell sat idle for two years. We took over supervision of the remaining construction, vetted contractors, and brought it to a leasable state in four months.

"They caught a contractor overbilling us before a single extra dollar left the account." Property investor, based in the UK

RIA Road Duplex

Rent was being collected informally with no paper trail. We placed vetted tenants, formalized leases, and now remit collected rent with a financial statement every month.
